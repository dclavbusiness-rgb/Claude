Coverage

Health & Wellness Benefits

Comprehensive coverage begins on your first day of employment. NOKO covers the majority of premiums so you and your family are protected.

🏥

Medical Insurance

BlueCross BlueShield PPO & HDHP plans

  • NOKO covers 80% of employee premium
  • NOKO covers 50% of dependent premiums
  • PPO: $500 individual / $1,000 family deductible
  • HDHP: $1,500 individual / $3,000 family deductible
  • Out-of-pocket max: $4,000 / $8,000
  • Coverage effective Day 1 of employment
🦷

Dental Insurance

Delta Dental

  • Preventive care (cleanings, X-rays) at 100%
  • Basic restorative (fillings) at 80%
  • Major restorative (crowns, bridges) at 50%
  • Orthodontia: $1,500 lifetime benefit
  • Annual maximum benefit: $2,000 per person
  • NOKO covers 75% of premium
👁️

Vision Insurance

VSP Vision Care

  • Annual eye exam fully covered (in-network)
  • $200 frames/lenses allowance per year
  • $150 contact lens allowance per year
  • 15% discount on non-covered frames
  • LASIK discount program available
  • NOKO covers 75% of premium
💳

Health Savings Account (HSA)

Available with HDHP enrollment — Fidelity HSA

  • NOKO contributes $600/year (individual) or $1,200/year (family)
  • 2026 IRS contribution limits: $4,300 / $8,550
  • Funds roll over year to year — never lost
  • Triple tax advantage (contribute, grow, spend tax-free)
  • Invest HSA funds once balance exceeds $1,000
  • Portable — yours even if you leave NOKO
💊

Prescription Drug Coverage

Included with all medical plans

  • Tier 1 generics: $10 copay
  • Tier 2 preferred brand: $35 copay
  • Tier 3 non-preferred brand: $60 copay
  • Tier 4 specialty: 20% coinsurance
  • 90-day mail-order option available
  • Rx out-of-pocket max included in medical max
🧠

Mental Health & EAP

Included with all medical plans + standalone EAP

  • 6 free therapy sessions per year via EAP
  • Mental health parity — same as medical coverage
  • Free access to Calm premium subscription
  • Telehealth mental health visits: $0 copay
  • Substance use treatment covered
  • Confidential — HR never sees usage

📋 Open enrollment happens each November for a January 1 effective date. New hires may enroll within 30 days of their start date. Questions? Email benefits@nokobenefits.com

Rest & Recharge

Paid Time Off Policy

We believe rest makes better work. Take the time you need to show up fully.

Vacation / PTO

15 days / year
Years 1–2
18 days / year
Years 3–5
22 days / year
Years 6+
  • Accrues bi-weekly starting on Day 1
  • Up to 5 days can roll over annually
  • PTO payout upon separation (where required by law)
🤒

Sick Leave

8 days per year

  • Separate from PTO — use it without guilt
  • Can be used for family member care
  • Rolls over up to 16 days total
  • No doctor's note required for 3 days or fewer
🎉

Company Holidays

11 paid holidays per year

  • New Year's Day
  • Martin Luther King Jr. Day
  • Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day
  • Thanksgiving Day + day after
  • Christmas Eve + Christmas Day
  • 2 floating holidays (your choice)
👶

Parental Leave

Up to 12 weeks paid

  • Primary caregiver: 12 weeks fully paid
  • Secondary caregiver: 6 weeks fully paid
  • Applies to birth, adoption, and foster placement
  • Eligible after 6 months of employment
  • Can be taken within 12 months of birth/placement
🕯️

Bereavement Leave

Up to 5 days paid

  • 5 days for immediate family (spouse, child, parent, sibling)
  • 3 days for extended family (grandparents, in-laws)
  • Additional unpaid leave available upon request
⚖️

Other Leave

  • Jury Duty: Full pay for up to 10 days
  • Military Leave: Per USERRA requirements
  • Voting: 2 hours paid leave on Election Day
  • Volunteer: 8 hours paid volunteer time per year
  • FMLA: 12 weeks unpaid per federal law

Your Future

401(k) Retirement Plan

We invest in your long-term future — because great people deserve great retirements.

4% Company match on your contributions
Day 1 Immediate vesting on all company contributions
90 days Eligibility window after hire date
🏦

Plan Details

  • Traditional (pre-tax) and Roth (post-tax) options
  • Administered by Fidelity Investments
  • 100% match on first 4% of salary contributed
  • 2026 IRS limit: $23,500 / $31,000 (age 50+)
  • Immediate vesting — no waiting period
  • Eligible after 90 days of employment
📊

Investment Options

  • 30+ mutual funds and index funds available
  • Target-date funds for hands-off investing
  • ESG / socially responsible fund options
  • Automatic rebalancing available
  • Self-directed brokerage window (BrokerageLink)
📚

Financial Wellness

  • Free 1:1 sessions with a Fidelity advisor
  • Online financial planning tools included
  • Retirement readiness score tracker
  • Annual group financial wellness workshops
  • Student loan repayment planning resources

💡 Pro tip: Contribute at least 4% to get the full company match — that's free money toward your retirement. Contact Fidelity at 1-800-343-3548 or visit netbenefits.com to enroll or update contributions.

How to Enroll

Set Up Your Schedule

Go to Schedulefly →

Go to Toast →

Go to HealthiestYou →
